## flexBART 2.0.0

### Major changes

- Add support for varying coefficient models (i.e., multipe additve
  ensembles)
- Remove unnecessary passes over the full training data during
  backfitting and computing conditional posterior of leaf nodes
- Introduce pre-processing functions
- Add support for nested categorical features
- Introduce formula interface
